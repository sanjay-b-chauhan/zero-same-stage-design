## 2. Meeting notes

**When the panel appears.** Not during `pre-intro` (segment 0 plays alone, columns `1fr 0fr`). At the end of segment 0, if the next segment carries a `context_point_id` (Spotify: Problem does), phase becomes `intro` (`shouldIntroduceNotesPanel`). Player bar hides, audio is paused. `useNotesPanelSegmentRule` also forces any note-bearing segment out of pre-intro on entry. The panel is `rounded-[24px] bg-white border border-white overflow-hidden`.

**Notes intro card** (TS/components/NotesPanelIntroCard.tsx, variant `card`). 140ms after intro mounts the column opens (550ms) and the panel slides x 64 to 0 with spring (stiffness 70, damping 14) plus 300ms opacity; a slide sound plays. Card content, scaled .65 and lifted 67px, lines rise with blur 10 to 0 and y 35 to 0, 0.7s each, stagger .11s at orders 0, 2, 4, 6:
- cream circle 150px `#EDEBE6` with the notes icon 58px at 80%
- title "Meeting Notes", STK Bureau serif 56px, tracking -1.68px, ink `#433D40`
- subtitle "Earn xp by noting all the important details of the project", Google Sans Flex 24px medium, 60% opacity, width 366px
- button "I'm ready": 90px high, `px-[60px]`, `rounded-[150px]`, bg `#1F1D1E`, 30px semibold white, spring press.
Click "I'm ready": circle, subtitle and button exit (350ms, staggered .1s); the icon and the title fly into the panel header slots on a 700ms bezier (title starts +100ms and lands with the icon); at 686ms a 216px white blurred glow pulses (1050ms keyframe) and the card corner takes an elastic tug (-6, -5 px, hit 110ms, release 780ms); header icon and title appear at 700ms; `onGotIt` fires at `max(880, 686+1050+40) = 1776ms`. Then `handleBegin`: connects the note-checker socket, phase `playing`, advances to segment 1 and its audio starts. So roughly 1.8s between the click and the next narration.

**Panel anatomy** (TS/components/NotesPanel.tsx, ChecklistNoteItem.tsx). Header strip `min-h-[52px] px-6 py-3.5 bg-[#EDEBE6]`: notes icon 18px plus "Meeting Notes" (`text-sm font-medium text-[#433D40]`). Body `px-8 py-6`, scrolls, bottom padding `55vh`. Per segment: heading = segment label (Problem, Outcome, Process) in `text-base font-semibold` Google Sans Flex `#2A2118`, `pb-3`, then a 1px `border-gray-200` rule `mt-3`. Rows `space-y-1`, each `gap-3`, text `text-sm leading-relaxed`; bullet column 20px. Only segments with context points get a section and an editor (`requireContextPoints`); intro and outro have none. Earlier segments stay above, read-only; auto-scroll to the current heading.

**Does Zero write notes for you?** The learner types everything. A note-bearing segment opens with one blank row already in it. Placeholders: "Take a note..." (`text-sm text-gray-300`, shown only when the segment has zero rows) and "Write here..." in the textarea. Keys: Enter adds a row (Shift+Enter newline); ArrowUp/Down move between rows at the text boundary; Backspace on an empty row deletes it and focuses the previous. A soft `noteAdded` sound as each bullet gains text. The ONLY text Zero writes is after grading: every context point the learner did not cover is appended as a `missed` row (text = the point description from the grader) with a black star icon (14px) and a decrypt-scramble reveal (DecryptedText). Em dashes are scrubbed.

**What triggers the check.** At the end of a note-bearing segment the phase becomes `segment-pause` and a dark card sits on the seekbar at the segment's end position (centred on `segEnd%`, `bottom-full mb-3`, scales in from 0 with y 15 over 280ms; leaves over 220ms). `SegmentPopIn`: `bg-[#1F1D1E] rounded-[28px] px-8 py-7 w-[280px] shadow-2xl`, 48px facilitator avatar, text "Are we clear?" (`text-base font-medium`), buttons Yes (white pill) and Ask (`white/10` pill; only if a socket client is passed, which the harness does), link "Watch again" (`text-sm white/60`, replays the segment from its start). No minimize control, no keyboard dismiss. Any seek while the card is up, including "Watch again", routes through `handleChapterSeek`, which minimizes the card to a pulsing dot on the seekbar (24px hit area; click restores) and unminimizes it when the replay reaches the segment end (the code comment says the card stays up; the behaviour is the dot).
- Ask opens `SegmentNpcChatPopup` (360px wide, 360px tall chat, `bg-[#1B1A1A] rounded-[28px]`, header button "Continue", input placeholder "Type here..."). In the harness she answers in 900ms from the segment's context points.
- Yes runs `useKickoffNotesCheck`. For a note-bearing segment with a scenario id it first sends coach event `kickoff_notes_done` `{notes_count, segment label, segment_index}` and waits for the reply (`busy` disables Yes at 50% opacity). If the coach shows a line (`shown: true`) this Yes is swallowed once and the learner must press Yes again (`stoppedOn`). If the coach link is absent, errors, or says nothing, it goes straight on. The standalone has no coach link, so it never blocks.
- Then `submitSegmentNotes`: phase `analyzing`, WebSocket `note_checker` frame `{action:'submit_segment', user_id, segment_id, scenario_id, attempt_id, event_key:'evt-{user}-{uuid}', points:[{id,text}...]}`. Double submit is guarded. A retry reuses the same event_key (backend ledger replays instead of regrading).

**What the check returns.** Frames: `processing_status` (202), then one `point_result` per note `{id,text,status: correct|incorrect|duplicate, xp, matched:[{context_point_id,line_id}]}`, `performance_result` per correct note (triggers the XP bubble flight), then `segment_result` `{points (adds status missed with context_point_id), summary{required,hit,missed,submitted,correct,incorrect}, total_xp, completion_acknowledgement}`. `completion_acknowledgement` is parsed but its copy is commented out of the card, so nothing renders it.

**What the learner sees while it runs.**
- Card swaps to the `analyzing` view: avatar plus a pill shimmer with the single word "Analyzing" (2s shimmer loop). Player bar stays pinned and disabled.
- Notes: an amber pulsing dot and amber text step through the pending notes at 800ms per note, looping, until each note's real result arrives.
- Each `point_result` is queued and revealed 1200ms apart (`POINT_REVEAL_DELAY_MS`). Correct: green stroked 20px circle expands (300ms), tick draws (350ms), `+{xp}` pops above (17.5px bold, floats 16px up and fades over 650ms), then an XP star flies to the top-left `LevelBadge` (pitched `xpEarned` sound rising with streak; `xpCollected`, meter hit sound, meter pop). Incorrect: red X circle icon (`#FE4938`), text turns plain grey (the line-through is commented out). Missed: star plus scramble.
- `segment_result` waits until the point queue drains. Once the grade is applied and the XP batch fired, the phase moves to `results` after 300ms (2500ms if bubbles are still flying; 8000ms hard watchdog; 300ms if there is no panel).
- `results` view: avatar plus one white pill "Continue" (full width of the card). Continue returns to `playing` and starts the next segment, or to `sync-check` after the last.

**Weak or empty note sets.** Nothing gates the submit. Zero notes, one note, or junk all go through. The grader returns incorrect/duplicate for bad notes and fills every uncovered point as missed rows. There is no minimum, no re-ask loop, no "try again" for thin notes, and no threshold text. The only soft interruption is the backend-decided coach moment above. Failures: a socket error or an unapplyable result sends the phase back to `segment-pause` and the card shows "We're having some kind of trouble right now" with a "Try again" pill (the specific `analysisError` string, 'We could not show your results. Your notes were graded, so hit Retry to load them.', is stored but not rendered). Slowness has no deadline: a silent re-ask after 60s up to 3 times (`NOTE_GRADE_REASK_MS`); the helper copy "Checking your notes…" (shown from 5s) and "Taking longer than usual" followed by "still working on it." (from 20s; the source joins them with an em dash) exists in TS/utils/noteGradingProgress.ts but is wired only to plan mode, not here.

**Harness timings for the check** (SA/mocks.ts). Socket open 60ms, auth ack 80ms, `processing_status` 260ms, first `point_result` at 900ms (`gradeDelay`), then every 1200ms (`gradeGapMs`), `performance_result` 550ms after each correct, `segment_result` at `900 + n*1200 + 700` ms. XP 10 per point. Three notes land the result at 5200ms; with the 1200ms client reveal spacing the card changes to Continue about 5.5 to 6s after Yes.

## 3. Transition: meeting to POU

1. Outro (last segment, no context points) ends. No pop-in. Phase `sync-check`. Right column shows `SyncCheckBar`: `bg-[#1F1D1E] rounded-4xl px-6 py-5`, text "I hope you're completely in sync" (`text-base font-medium` white) and a white pill "Yes, totally" (`px-6 py-2.5 text-sm`). Media is silenced; the bar and Space still work (clicking play restarts from segment 0 for a replay).
2. Meanwhile, when the final segment starts (fresh sessions only), `sendStart()` already fired, so her first POU question is generated and stored before the chat mounts.
3. Click "Yes, totally": `completeMeeting` (once only), stops audio and video, `onMeetingComplete(notes)`. Parent: `sequencer.stop()`, `stopAllPlayback()`, saves notes, marks note-checker training complete (once), sets `nativeMeterPhase = 'conceal'`, raises `CoinMeterBridge`, `setSubView('sync-check-chat')`.
4. Body swap (500ms, curve `[0.32,0.72,0,1]`): meeting layer exits with `leave`: player column AND notes column each go opacity 0 and scale .96 (the old horizontal slide and blur were removed). Chat layer enters opacity 0 to 1, scale .98 to 1, delay 60ms. Other layers exit with duration 0. The top bar, its back button and pills never move.
5. What persists: top bar, level badge, performance chip, header pill, page ground `#F2F0ED`, the right-hand notes slot (now a read-only copy). What leaves: video/deck, player bar, captions, editable notes, pop-ins. The understanding meter appears in the bar centre but is laid out invisible (opacity 0, "conceal") so the bridge can measure it.
6. Held back during the bridge: the chat column is `opacity 0` (400ms ease) with an empty message list, so no voice can mount or autoplay; the read-only notes column does not mount (`meterConcealed`).
7. `CoinMeterBridge` starts after `BRIDGE_START_DELAY_MS = (0.5 + 0.12) * 1000 = 620ms`, waits for the concealed meter to be measurable (after 4000ms it gives up and just shows the plain meter; reduced motion also skips the bridge and "Got it" entirely). A lime wash (`radial-gradient` at top, 600ms in, 260ms out) tints the page and a full-screen click shield blocks the page. The coin (24 fps replica of "Brain meter V4", 49 frames ≈ 2042ms): pops scale 0, 1.32, .911, 1 (25 frames ≈ 1042ms) with a 16 spark burst (900 to 1400ms each, removed at 1600ms) and glow, flips twice (23 frames ≈ 958ms), expands to the capsule (29 frames ≈ 1208ms, starts frame 19 ≈ 792ms), brain moves from centre to the capsule end and recolours `#49BA61` to `rgba(31,29,30,.6)`. At `49*41.67 + 60 ≈ 2100ms` after start, the copy appears and waits: serif "Context Meter" (56px scaled to meter width), "This shows your understanding of the context." (24px, 60%), white button "Got it" (60px high pill, springs). It waits for the click with no timeout.
8. Click "Got it": the capsule glides uniformly into the real meter (550ms `cubic-bezier(0.42,0,0.58,1)`, glow fades 260ms), `finish` at 590ms: real meter takes over at identical pixels, `firstVoiceHeld = false`.
9. Release: chat column fades in (400ms); read-only notes panel slides in x 28 to 0 over 450ms (shown only if there are visible notes: segments with points plus anything written; code shows all non-empty notes including incorrect ones although a comment says it hides them). The pre-warmed first question mounts as a loader bubble that grows into the playable note and autoplays.
Dead time before her first word, derived: 500ms swap, 620ms hold, about 2.1s coin, the learner's click, about 0.6s glide, then the note morph. A re-entry that lands directly in the POU (resume) skips the bridge and the "Got it" screen entirely.

## 4. Kickoff POU voice note exchange

**Layout** (KM/Chat/index.tsx 741 to 905). Body `grid pt-[58px] lg:pt-[66px] px-6 lg:px-10 pb-6 lg:pb-10 gap-x-[30px]`; columns `1.8fr 0.8fr` (notes available) or `1fr` (500ms ease-in-out). Chat inner `max-w-3xl mx-auto px-6` (768px), scroll area with a mask (top 16px fade, bottom 40px fade) and a 15% `#F2F0ED` top fade after 30px scroll, bottom spacer `h-28` (112px). Mic block `pb-5`, `max-w-3xl`. Her bubbles left, learner's right.

**States in order.**
1. Resume skeleton (only if a session already exists): three grey bubbles plus a 64px mic circle, `animate-pulse`, until the resume frame returns.
2. Her question. A loader pill (60x38, three wave bars plus two tail circles, "breathing") morphs by GSAP over 0.6s (`power3.inOut`) into the note pill (width ≈ 255.6, height 62, blur crossfade, then +0.4s settle). Note row: 40px avatar, white pill, 42px play circle (`rgba(42,33,24,.1)`, glyph `#747474`, 27px), 18 waveform bars (3.15px wide, 3.82 gap, max 28px; unplayed `rgba(42,33,24,.4)`, played `#2A2118`), 56px timer column. Time shows the full duration (`mm:ss`, rounded up) when idle and elapsed while playing. While playing the time shrinks to .77 scale and a speed pill "x1" appears under it (11px, `rgba(31,29,30,.05)`, radius 13, height animates 0.36s); click cycles 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2 and is shared by all notes (store, localStorage `kickoff-voice-playback-speed`). Waveform is click-to-seek. Autoplays once when audio loads (unless it is already playing as a live stream); starting any note or recording pauses the others (`audioPlaybackCoordinator`). A second line under the pill: toggle "transcript" (12px, uppercase by CSS, 50% ink, chevron), which unfolds the text (200ms, 14px, max `70cqw`). Her multi-part replies merge into one note with parts joined by a blank line. If audio never comes (`voiceUnavailable`) or fails, her text shows as a `rounded-[18px] bg-[#00000008]` 16px bubble.
3. Idle mic (`TS/Chat/HoldToTalk.tsx`): glass pill 124x74, radius 55, fill `white/16` plus-lighter blend, large soft shadow; mic glyph 24px. Label under it, mono 14px uppercase tabular, 50% ink: "HOLD 'SPACE' TO TALK". The whole control scales `clamp(.6, 1, innerWidth/1920)`. Disabled while she is working: `(isWaitingForNpc && !npcTurnSettled) || pendingSend`.
4. Recording: pointer down on the mic or Space keydown (not repeat, not when a text field or modal has focus). Everything else stops playing (`stopAllPlayback`). The pill grows 124 to 536 over 0.42s (`[0.22,1,0.36,1]`) while contents crossfade with 10px blur (0.28s). Contents: cancel 50px circle (X), live waveform of 48 bars (rAF, a sample every 70ms, bar 4 to 24px, ink `#1F1D1E`), countdown `mm:ss` counting DOWN from 02:00 (14px tabular, red `#E5484D` at 10s or less), send 50px dark circle `#1F1D1E` (inset top highlight). Label "RECORDING" with three bouncing dots. Release (pointer up/leave or Space keyup) sends; Escape or X cancels. Holds under 1000ms (counted from the press, not from mic ready) are silently dropped. Max 120s (auto stop). Silent take (peak below .05): pill returns and a red alert shows "Can't hear you" / "Check your system settings." Mic that does not open in 15s: "Your microphone took too long to start. Please try again." A stuck recorder resets after 3000ms. `playPouRecordStartSound` on start, `playSendSound` on release.
5. Send morph: record pill shrinks to scale .35, blur 24px, opacity 0 over 0.8s while the learner's card grows in (spring, bounce .3, duration 1.0s, from scale .36 at origin 100% 50%). ## 6. Must not lose

- **Resume and restore.** localStorage `kickoff-reload-snapshot:{userId}:{scenarioId}` `{version:1, subView, meetingPhase, currentSegmentIndex, currentSegmentId, updatedAt}` written on every phase and segment change; back-end note-checker progress also restores graded notes. Re-entering an in-progress meeting skips the landing card and auto-resumes silently (no chime) at the right segment: `segment-pause`/`analyzing` replay that segment from its start, `results` or already-graded moves to the next segment, `sync-check` stays. Landing CTA reads "Continue" when resumable, "Join Now" otherwise. Initial subview priority: completed, then in-progress meeting snapshot (beats a pre-warmed POU unless the backend says complete), then POU with a real learner turn, then landing. A reload during the last segment must not skip to the POU.
- **Back from the POU** goes to the "pou-landing" screen (mono "Kickoff", serif "Ready to check your understanding of the kickoff meeting?", 80px facilitator avatar with a green presence dot, "Continue"), not to the meeting; Continue re-enters the chat (no coin bridge, no intro). Back from the meeting exits to the scenario home after the confirm. The POU chat mounts its own copy of the same exit dialog, but with `hideHeader` set its only trigger (the chat header) is not rendered, so in practice Back is the shared top bar and it goes to pou-landing without a confirm.
- **Ungraded runs** (no segment has a context point): every segment except the first takes notes, nothing is submitted for grading, notes persist in storage (`ungradedNotesKey`).
- **Sounds** (all through `sfx` or Web Audio, all decorative): join chime, kickoff open (once per user and scenario), note appears, XP earned (pitch steps up with the streak, resets on a miss), XP collected, meter hit (steps up one semitone per landing), notes panel slide, notes header settle, brain meter appear and text, POU record start, send, analysing loop, emoji pop, praise, orb launch, bar grow, neutral. Recording stops every other player. No background music.
- **Pointer and focus.** Controls fold after 3s idle and return on any move, including moves inside the deck iframe. Space never toggles the player while a field is focused or a modal is open. During POU, Space is the record key, so the meeting's Space handler is gone with the meeting.
- **Reduced motion.** Springs and slides collapse to short opacity fades; the coin flight and notes flight are skipped (straight hand-off); the understanding bar fills in about 1ms and loses the shimmer.
- **Safety nets already in the code.** Meeting audio and video are paused when the phase becomes `intro` or `sync-check` and again on unmount, so nothing bleeds into the POU. Chat auto-scroll re-pins at 60, 280, 600ms (new note) and 120, 450, 850ms (reply resolved) because the notes grow after layout. A turn that never gets an answer frees the mic after 60s. A resume while a turn is in flight is merged, never duplicated (`event_key` + `message_part` keys; restored turns never autoplay). Duplicate frames are ignored.
- **Do not copy** the legacy gate path (`show_gate`, `zero_gate_closed`) or the stage PoP markers; `LEGACY_HTML_GATES_ENABLED = false`, so a deck has no gates and no seekbar marks in kickoff.
- **Telemetry and presence** are invisible (`useModuleTiming('kickoff')`, `usePresenceModule`, playback events, `trackFeature` start/complete, menu bar activity). No UI.
